# ADR-032: Dashboard display of official IMF WEO projections (lifts the ADR-011 v2 deferral for one slice; amends ADR-010 decision 9, ADR-030 decision 7, export spec principle 4)

Date: 2026-11-07. Labels: [REPO] read in this repository on 2026-11-07; [REC] stated in `docs/ops/dashboard-redesign-decisions.md`; [DOC] read on the primary page this session (through the fetch tool's summary, wording not read in full); [UNVERIFIED] not checked. Access date for every URL: 2026-11-07.

## Context
- The user's goal for the redesign was a dashboard that tells an economist something, with forecasts where the design allows them [REC]. The record found that no approved document allows them in the redesign release: ADR-010 decision 9 ("Projections (A03) have no dashboard output in v1"), export spec principle 4 (`PROJ` excluded from every file, including the revision files), ADR-011 (forecast storage approved but "deferred to v2 by user decision 2026-10-25; approval does not schedule implementation"), ADR-030 decision 7 and Q5 (no projections, no projections sentence), and ADR-010 decision 1 / D6 (no computation in the browser, which rules out browser-side extrapolation) [REC, REPO].
- What exists: IMF WEO A03 and the projection years of other WEO-fed indicators (A04, A05, A25) are modelled as rows with `value_status = 'PROJ'` and a `vintage_label` per edition (for example `WEO-2026-10`), loaded from manual downloads (research decision D23, `manual_inbox/`); under the interim rule of ADR-002 (I-CUR-3) every edition stores a row, even when unchanged. PROJ rows are excluded from ranks, alerts, revisions and exports [REPO: ADR-011, export spec principle 4].
- ADR-011 proposes a separate `forecast` schema (`forecast.edition`, `forecast.projection`, `forecast.evaluation`) for the full forecasting amendment (own-model nowcasts, evaluation, probability alerts). Only the edition and projection tables are needed to display an official projection.
- Licence facts (IMF terms of use, https://www.imf.org/en/About/Copyright-and-Terms, accessed 2026-11-07, [DOC] via a page summary): for statistical data (named there: IFS, WEO database) users may "download, extract, copy, create derivative works, publish, distribute, and use Data" under conditions; distributed data "must appear accurately with attribution to the IMF as the source", example form "Source: International Monetary Fund, Database Name, <<link to the dataset>>"; "if material transformation occurs, it must be stated explicitly along with the required source citation"; for any potential commercial reuse permission is requested from copyright@imf.org; the IMF "prohibits bulk download of information by automated technology without explicit permission". The IMF's publications (the WEO report text) are under a stricter regime (non-systematic personal non-commercial use, no redistribution; written permission otherwise). The page shows no effective date. Whether the WEO database files are "Statistical Data" in the sense of that section for our use is my reading of the summary, [UNVERIFIED] against the full page text. The site is non-commercial (ADR-010 decision 7, prohibited-use clause).
- Existing gate: the `publish_site` field per source (`public` or `withheld`, `config/sources.yaml`, rule 15 of the source-config schema) already controls redistribution, with a leak test (AC-EXP-4). The current value for the WEO manual source entry was not checked in this session [UNVERIFIED].

## Options considered
A. Store: (1) export `PROJ` rows from `curated.observation` (interim rule) through a new published view, no new schema. Fewest moving parts now, but ADR-011 retires the interim rule when it is implemented, every consumer of the observation view must keep filtering `PROJ`, and the observation key has no edition-level evidence of its own. (2) Implement only the edition and projection tables of ADR-011 (`forecast.edition`, `forecast.projection`) for the producer `IMF_WEO`, point values only, with a published view for the export; evaluation, other producers, own-model and probability targets stay deferred (chosen). Costs one migration and a loader change, but follows the approved ADR-011 design and keeps two different keys out of one table.
B. Display: (1) projections inside the observed series files and charts with a status marker. Rejected: contradicts the separation of observed and projected data and risks misreading [REC, ADR-030 Q5]. (2) A separate file and a separate visual layer on PG-SERIES only (chosen). (3) Also on PG-COUNTRY and PG-INDICATOR. Rejected for step 1: those pages show percentiles, ranks and a common reference period; a projection has none of these and mixing it would breach "never mixed into percentiles, ranks, alerts or the value chart" [REC].
C. Producers: (1) only official third-party projections from the IMF WEO (chosen). (2) Also OECD, EC, ECB projections. Out of scope; each needs its own licence check. (3) Own-model nowcasts or trend extrapolation. Out of scope; needs forecast evaluation next to it (ADR-011 `forecast.evaluation`) and a later ADR [REC].
D. Do nothing; keep ADR-010 decision 9 as is. Valid and cheap; the dashboard then stays observed-only.

## Decision
Proposed, not scheduled. All of the following apply only if the user approves this ADR.

1. Scope. Step 1 displays official IMF WEO projections only: point values of the latest loaded edition, on PG-SERIES only. No other producer, no own-model forecast, no trend extrapolation, no fan charts or intervals (none are stored), no display on PG-COUNTRY, PG-INDICATOR, the domain heatmaps or the revisions pages. Projections never enter percentiles, ranks, alerts, n/N, the value chart or any scorecard (the D6 rule stays: nothing is computed in the browser).
2. Preconditions (all must hold before a plan is approved).
   a. The user approves this ADR.
   b. Licence confirmation by the user (ADR-010 decision 7 gate): the user confirms, after reading the IMF terms page, that publishing WEO database projections on this non-commercial public site with the attribution of decision 7 is permitted; if the user wants a written confirmation, the e-mail route named by the IMF is the user's action, not an agent's. The WEO source entry must carry `publish_site: public`; if it is `withheld` today, the user changes it explicitly. If the terms do not permit it, this ADR is not implemented.
   c. The ingestion route stays the existing manual download (`manual_inbox/`, ADR-005): the IMF's prohibition of automated bulk download is respected by construction. Any plan to automate WEO downloads needs a separate ADR and IMF permission.
   d. The slice of ADR-011 in decision 3 is scheduled by the user.
3. Storage (lifts the ADR-011 v2 deferral for this slice only). Implement `forecast.edition` and `forecast.projection` as specified in ADR-011 for `producer = 'IMF_WEO'`, `value_kind = 'point'`: edition-based, every edition stores every value it publishes, append-only, same privilege rules as `curated.observation`. `forecast.evaluation`, the model layer, probability targets and the benchmark-archive import remain deferred. The interim rule (I-CUR-3) and `load_kind = 'import'` handling of ADR-002 and ADR-011 retire for WEO when the slice lands: existing PROJ rows are migrated by a forward-only migration into the new tables (the old rows are never updated or deleted, CLAUDE.md). A published view `forecast.v_projection_published` (only `publish_site = public` sources, latest edition per series, point values) is the only object the export reads for projections, under `mm_reader`; ADR-012's privilege matrix gains that one view. Exact DDL belongs to the design round that follows approval, and is not specified here. ADR-011 gets an amendment note (deferral lifted for this slice).
   If the user prefers Option A(1) instead, decisions 4 to 9 are unchanged; only this decision and the migration work differ, and ADR-011's interim-rule retirement is postponed.
4. Export (breaks the contract: manifest `schema_version` 2.0, a major bump; the front end refuses an unknown major, so export and site deploy together). Amend `docs/specs/dashboard-export-spec.md`:
   a. Principle 4: "Projections (`PROJ`) are excluded from every file except `projection/<indicator_id>.json`". Revision files, scorecards, alerts, `series/*` and `revisions_recent.json` remain projection-free.
   b. New file `projection/<indicator_id>.json`, one per indicator that has at least one exportable projection; listed in `manifest.files` with bytes and sha256 like every file. Content: `producer` (`IMF_WEO`), `edition_label` (for example `WEO-2026-10`), `published_at` (edition date, ISO date), `known_at` (our fetch or load time), and `rows[]` of `{entity_id, series_id, target_period_start, target_period_end, frequency, value, value_status: "PROJ"}`. Unit, seasonal adjustment and source name come from `meta/series.json` via `series_id`, as for every series (CLAUDE.md mandatory metadata: source, series id, frequency, unit, seasonal adjustment, vintage). Sorted keys, deterministic bytes (principle 5). Estimated size is small (a few hundred rows per indicator at most) [analyst estimate, unmeasured]; measured under AC-EXP budgets before release.
   c. `meta/indicators.json` gains the nullable boolean `projection_available`, set by analytics or config, never derived in the browser.
   d. Only the latest loaded edition is exported in step 1. Earlier editions stay in the store; an edition-comparison view is a later decision.
   e. EXP-02 is split: no `PROJ` value in any file except `projection/*`; every row of `projection/*` has `value_status = PROJ`. A new check EXP-11: every projection row's source has `publish_site = public` (perturbation test as AC-EXP-4).
5. Comparability rule. A projection is exported only for an (indicator, entity) pair where the user has marked the indicator `projection_comparable: true` in `config/indicators.yaml` (default false). Reason: WEO definitions can differ from the headline series (ADR-011 records `definition_note` for average versus end-period inflation, fiscal-year versus calendar-year, e.g. India, Australia, Japan in A04 [REPO: research indicator-selection A04 note]); the site must not draw a projection as the continuation of a series it does not continue. Step 1 is limited to annual frequency (A03 is annual); monthly or quarterly headline series get no projection layer. Which indicators are marked is the user's decision.
6. Display (data only; no explanatory prose, ADR-030 data-only rule). PG-SERIES, when a projection file exists for the plotted indicator and country:
   a. A separate layer, off by default, switched by a labelled checkbox from `copy.js` (DRAFT). It is available only for the Level transform without display-only rebase; under any other transform or rebase the layer is hidden and the checkbox is disabled (browser arithmetic on projections is not introduced; if the layer were rebased it would be a "material transformation" in the IMF sense and would need a statement; the design avoids it).
   b. Visual separation by non-colour channels first (ADR-025 decision 3): a dashed line, a vertical rule at the last observed period (the maximum period of the plotted OBS, EST or PROV points; selection only, no calculation), and a hollow diamond marker distinct from the hollow circle of estimates. The layer has its own legend entry and text code. Colour is a third channel only. The projection line never joins the observed line: the dashed segment starts at the first projected period.
   c. Label as data: "IMF WEO 2026-10" (producer plus edition label), not a sentence. Tip lines: country, period label, value with unit, the status label "Projection", edition label and `published_at` (YYYY-MM-DD). The point reader (spec amendment `dashboard-redesign-point-reader-amendment.md`) lists projected periods as well, tagged the same way.
   d. "View as table" gains a second, separate table, "Projections", with columns Country, Target period, Value, Unit, Source, Edition, Published. Observed tables never contain projected rows. The figure's `aria-label` names the projection layer when visible.
   e. No confidence bands, no interpolation, no extrapolation (ADR-025 decision 6 unchanged).
7. Source, vintage and attribution rules.
   a. Every projected value is shown with producer, edition label and edition date (`published_at`); the unit and seasonal adjustment as for every value; the `known_at` in "About these series".
   b. Attribution, shown whenever the projection layer is visible and in the footer sources line: `Source: International Monetary Fund, World Economic Outlook database, <edition label>, <link to the IMF WEO page>.` (form taken from the IMF example above; exact string DRAFT, user confirms; the link target is the user's choice and is checked at implementation, https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/WEO is the page cited by ADR-020 [REPO]).
   c. Transformation statement: the site stores and shows the published values unchanged; no unit conversion, no rebase, no rounding beyond the `published_precision` display hint. If this ever changes, the statement must say so explicitly (IMF terms).
   d. The IMF WEO report text is not copied; only database values are shown.
   e. Vintage: only one edition is shown at a time and it is named; the page-level data cut-off is unchanged. A projection is never described as a forecast by this site (it is the IMF's), and the site never adds its own interpretation.
8. Amendments to approved documents (not applied by this ADR; applied after approval, each as a separate edit that resets the document to `draft`): ADR-010 decision 9 (sentence "Projections (A03) have no dashboard output in v1" gains "except as ADR-032 permits on PG-SERIES"); ADR-011 (note: deferral lifted for the IMF_WEO point slice); ADR-030 decision 7 and Q5 (superseded by this ADR once approved); export spec principle 4, sections 2, 3, 4, 7, EXP-02 and the new EXP-11; `dashboard-redesign-spec.md` section 5.4, section 7 (the withdrawn string stays withdrawn: no projections sentence), CR-2 and AC-RD-44 (revised to: no page other than PG-SERIES reads `projection/*`; no `PROJ` value drawn outside the projection layer; no projections sentence); ADR-025 decision 3 (state vocabulary gains the projection signature) and AC-UX-2 (the signature is pairwise distinct from all existing states).
9. Sequencing. Not part of the current redesign release or its plan. After approval, a plan is written separately; the redesign ships first, observed-only, with the data-only rule and the 1.x contract.

## Acceptance criteria (new; test method)
- AC-PROJ-1 Separation in files. A schema test over a fixture export asserts: `PROJ` appears only in `projection/*`; every row of `projection/*` is `PROJ`; `series/*`, `scorecard/*`, `alerts.json`, `revisions/*` and `revisions_recent.json` contain none (EXP-02 split). (unit, golden files)
- AC-PROJ-2 Contract. `projection/<indicator_id>.json` validates against its schema; all files are in `manifest.files` with correct bytes and sha256; `schema_version` is `2.x`; the front end refuses an unknown major; two exports of the same inputs are byte-identical. (unit, golden)
- AC-PROJ-3 Publication gate. A perturbation test (as AC-EXP-4): setting the WEO source to `publish_site = withheld` removes all `projection/*` files and `projection_available` flags; no projection value appears anywhere. (db or fixture)
- AC-PROJ-4 Comparability. An indicator without `projection_comparable: true`, and a non-annual headline series, have no `projection/*` file. (unit)
- AC-PROJ-5 Visual separation. A smoke test on PG-SERIES asserts: the layer is off by default; when on, projection marks use the dashed style, the diamond marker, their own legend text code and a vertical rule at the last observed period; observed and projected points never share a line; under grayscale emulation the signature is distinguishable (extends AC-UX-2). (smoke)
- AC-PROJ-6 Exclusion elsewhere. No projection mark or value appears on PG-COUNTRY, PG-INDICATOR, heatmaps, revisions pages or in any percentile, rank, alert or n/N; a static scan lists the modules allowed to read `projection/*` (the series page modules only) and fails on others. (static scan, smoke)
- AC-PROJ-7 Level only. Under any non-Level transform or display-only rebase the layer is hidden and its control disabled; no projection value is arithmetically altered by any front-end code (extends the AC-RD-50 scan). (unit, scan)
- AC-PROJ-8 Provenance display. With the layer visible, the tip, the point reader and the "Projections" table all show producer, edition label and `published_at`, with the same text (equal content); the attribution string of decision 7b appears on the page and the footer. (smoke)
- AC-PROJ-9 Accessibility. The reader and the table cover every projected value; the figure `aria-label` names the layer; contrast and target size per AC-UX-1/4 for the new marks. (smoke)
- AC-PROJ-10 No new computation. The unit test of the tip text function (AC-RD-30a) extended with a PROJ case; the prose scan (AC-RD-62) passes with the new labels as listed `copy.js` entries. (unit)

## Consequences
- Positive: an economist sees the IMF's own view next to observed data, clearly separated, with edition and attribution; no own forecast and no browser computation; the redesign keeps its observed-only data contract until this is approved and scheduled; the work reuses ADR-011's approved design instead of a second store.
- Negative: a major contract bump (export, front end, tests move together); a migration and a loader change (new tables, backfill of existing PROJ rows); one more file type and a per-indicator comparability decision by the user; a visual layer with its own state signature, tooltip and table; licence reliance on the reading of the IMF terms, which the user must confirm; WEO is released twice a year and the manual download remains the ingestion route (the edition is only as fresh as the last download; freshness uses the edition basis of the freshness-state amendment).
- Risks: the licence summary was obtained through a page summary and carries no effective date [UNVERIFIED]; the terms may change; WEO definitions may not match the headline series for some indicators (mitigated by decision 5); readers may still treat a dashed line as the site's own forecast (mitigated by the producer/edition label and attribution, not by prose); the full ADR-011 store is a larger scope than this slice, and the boundary between them is the user's to confirm.
- Work created (after approval): export-spec amendment; a design round for the `forecast.edition`/`forecast.projection` slice DDL and loader change; indicator config key `projection_comparable`; PG-SERIES layer, tip, reader and table additions; new tests AC-PROJ-1 to 10; an ops note for the licence confirmation.
- Reversal: stop exporting `projection/*` and hide the layer; the stored tables stay (append-only) and ADR-010 decision 9 is restored by a superseding note.

A new CR is not opened; the export-spec amendment of decision 4 is the vehicle.
